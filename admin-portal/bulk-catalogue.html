Bulk Catalogue
Bulk products come from Zoho. To offer an item for bulk orders, tick the bulk checkbox on that item in Zoho — its rate, GST, HSN and unit are then used as-is, and orders can raise a Zoho sales order like any other. Products can't be added or edited here. What you set on this page is the minimum order value per category, which is what decides whether a customer can send a quote request at all.
